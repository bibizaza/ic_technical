# Full combination with the VIX re-entry rule F (information only)

All rules end 2026-08-31 (last C5 / macro-only pick month). Daily, 10 bp costs, cash = T-bill then BIL. V2 = R1 timing x volatility size. F = buy back to at least 50% while R1 is in cash, once VIX <= half its peak of the cash spell and < 30. K1 = V2 weight x (70% SPY + 3 x 10% sector ETFs). Macro-only = C5 without its intercept (M1). Code: `combo_f.py`.

F episodes on R1 (entry → end): 2009-05-19→2009-06-02, 2015-10-06→2015-11-03, 2016-03-29→2016-04-05, 2019-02-05→2019-03-05, 2020-05-26→2020-06-02, 2025-04-29→2025-06-03
